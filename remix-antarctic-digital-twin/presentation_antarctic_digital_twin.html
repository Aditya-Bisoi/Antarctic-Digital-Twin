Operational Challenge

The Extreme Realities of Antarctic Missions

Why traditional remote telemetry fails in the world's most inhospitable environment

❄️ Extreme Environmental Stress

  • Temperatures plummeting below -89°C with wind blizzards exceeding 100+ knots.
  • Sudden katabatic storms induce severe thermal dissipation on habitat modules.
  • Ice ablation and snow accumulation threaten structural vents and communication arrays.

⚡ Zero-Margin Life Support & Power

  • Stations operate as completely closed-loop electrical microgrids.
  • Generator tripping can drop indoor temperatures to sub-zero within hours, freezing water lines.
  • Fuel resupply is restricted to a narrow 3-month summer window; zero backup margin in winter.

🛰️ Communication Blackouts & Isolation

  • Total physical isolation for 8 to 9 months each year.
  • Solar radiation and polar geomagnetic storms disrupt satellite uplink bandwidth.
  • Demand for an autonomous edge-first digital twin that operates offline during communications loss.
System Architecture

End-to-End Architectural Blueprint

A modular 3-tier architecture ensuring sub-second visual updates and deterministic simulation

1. Frontend & 3D WebGL Tier

  • React 19 + TypeScript + Vite 6 for zero-lag reactive mission control.
  • Three.js + OrbitControls powering real-time 3D station models with realistic, thermal, and wireframe shaders.
  • IBCSO 71°S Polar Projection custom SVG stereographic map with weather isobar overlays.
  • Tailwind CSS v4 + Motion aerospace cockpit UI.

2. Physics & ML Intelligence Tier

  • Sub-Zero Thermal & Grid Simulator: Models battery drawdown curves, katabatic wind stress, and room temp decay.
  • Pure NumPy Vectorized ML: Lightweight Logistic & Ridge models running edge inference without heavy PyTorch/TensorFlow bloat.
  • Decision Support & Optimizer: Multi-objective automated power rationing & intervention sequencing.

3. Backend & Telemetry Persistence

  • Python 3.13 + Django 5.1 + DRF providing scalable REST endpoints.
  • Non-Mutating RPC Endpoint (`/api/stations/{id}/simulate/`) for instant hypothetical stress testing.
  • SQLite & Django ORM with seed telemetry databases for Maitri and Bharati subsystems.
  • JWT Authentication for secure operator session isolation.

Predictive ML

Zero-Overhead Predictive Maintenance Engine

Deployable on constrained station edge-servers without GPU requirements or cloud connectivity

⚡ 48-Hour Failure Probability

Logistic Regression with L2 Penalty:

  • Ingests vibration signatures, oil pressure, thermal variance, and running duty cycles.
  • Computes real-time equipment breakdown probability within the next 48 hours.
  • Enables proactive maintenance before catastrophic sub-zero seizure occurs.

⏳ Remaining Useful Life (RUL)

Vectorized Ridge Regression:

  • Evaluates continuous degradation metrics against historical wear baselines.
  • Accurately estimates remaining operational hours before turbine or pump failure.
  • Integrated into station spare parts supply forecast.

🚀 Why Pure NumPy?

Edge-Native Design Decision:

  • Zero heavy dependencies (No PyTorch, TensorFlow, or scikit-learn bloat).
  • Instant cold-start time (<10ms inference) and ultra-low RAM footprint.
  • Operates reliably on ruggedized edge computers and satellite micro-terminals.
